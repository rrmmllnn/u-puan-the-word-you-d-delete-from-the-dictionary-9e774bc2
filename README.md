## The word

**undo**

Delete "undo" and nobody can ever undo anything again — not a typo, not a
bad haircut, not even this deletion. The joke is the word itself: the gap
it leaves is the punchline, and the deletion can never be undone.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the single word `undo` to `/output/entry.txt` (4 bytes,
no trailing newline). It is the packed form of the readable source
`line_src.py`, packed with the competition kit's `makefun compress`.
